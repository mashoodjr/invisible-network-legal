Invisible Network

An automated publishing pipeline that turns trending video topics into finished, narrated clips — then delivers them to YouTube and TikTok for review.

What it does

Topic discovery

Surveys trending and popular videos across configured regions and categories — news, sports, technology, documentaries and reviews.

Clip production

Cuts highlights, adds spoken commentary, thumbnails, watermarking and subscribe cards — ready to publish.

Review-first uploads

Finished clips are uploaded privately to YouTube and to the TikTok drafts inbox, so every video is reviewed before it goes public.

Weekly recap

Automatically compiles the week's segments into a long-form recap episode every Sunday.

How the TikTok integration works

  1. Authorize. The app uses TikTok Login Kit (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE) to connect the owner's own TikTok account.
  2. Upload. Each day the highest-performing clip is re-cut to vertical format and sent through the Content Posting API (video.upload) to the account's TikTok inbox.
  3. Review & publish. The clip arrives as a draft; the owner edits, captions and publishes it manually in the TikTok app.

About

Invisible Network is a personal automation project operated by its owner for a single TikTok and YouTube account. It is not a public service and does not collect user data. For details, see the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
